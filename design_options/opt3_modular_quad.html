Ending during probation

Either party may end employment during probation subject to contract notice. Falcor provides at least 14 days' written notice; discussed in a formal review meeting and confirmed in writing.

14 Days Written Notice

Extensions & unpaid leave

Probation may be extended to a maximum total of six months, with Line Manager and HOD approval, confirmed in writing at least 2 weeks prior. Up to five days' approved unpaid leave extends probation accordingly.

HOD Approval Required
