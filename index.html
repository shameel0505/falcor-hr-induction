SAFETY · ADNOC & CLIENT SITES

Client Operating Site Protocols and Life-Saving Rules

🪪
When deployed to ADNOC, ADDC, or government client sites, you must complete the client's mandatory site induction before beginning work, and carry your Falcor ID and security site pass at all times.
🚫
ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED, EVER
  • No Smoking or Vaping: Strictly prohibited across plants; testing can occur at any time.
  • No Mobile Phones / Cameras: Non-intrinsically safe electronics strictly banned in live plant zones.
  • No Scope Creep: Never enter areas outside your permit scope, however convenient the shortcut.
  • No Material Removal: Never remove scrap, cables, or parts — even discarded items — without written pass.
  • Zero Substance Tolerance: Absolute zero tolerance for alcohol or drugs; testing is random.

  • Punctual Arrival: Arrive early enough to be at the work front in full PPE at shift start time.
  • Gate Passes & Road Safety: Display security passes; obey site speed limits and radar rules.
  • Vehicle Tracking (IVMS): Obey in-vehicle monitoring rules in company transport and site vans.
  • Professional Demeanor: No arguments with client staff or other contractors — escalate to supervisor.
  • Ask First: If you are not 100% certain something is allowed on site, ask before you do it.

YOUR EMPLOYMENT · LEAVE POLICIES

Returning from Leave on Time & Absence Protocols

🛬
You must return to duty on the very first working day following your approved leave. Operational continuity and client project commitments rely upon punctuality.
📅
Reporting Delays & Extensions
  • Expecting a delay due to flight cancellation or illness? Notify Line Manager and HR immediately
  • Provide official documentary evidence (airline delay certificate, hospital discharge paper)
  • In emergencies, a leave extension or grace period of up to 5 days must be approved before leave ends
  • If Falcor recalls you early, extra costs such as airfare difference are reimbursed against receipts
⚠
Lateness Without Prior Approval
  • Air ticket allowance benefit may be cancelled or recovered directly from monthly wages
  • Employee may be placed on unpaid leave status if temporary project replacement was deployed
  • First unexcused delay brings a formal written warning placed on employee record
  • Repeated unapproved delays disrupt client deliverables and lead to progressive termination
🚫
Unauthorized Absence (MOHRE)
  • Leave beyond approved duration, unexplained absence, or unjustified sick leave freezes pay immediately
  • UAE Labour Law Article 44: Unexplained absence over 7 consecutive days or 20 intermittent days is absconding
  • Results in immediate summary termination, loss of benefits, and reporting to immigration authorities

PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY PENALTIES
  • Penalty 1 (First Violation): Formal verbal warning, initial written warning, or administrative fine.
  • Penalty 2 (Second Violation): Final written warning, or forfeiting periodic salary increment / promotion.

GROSS MISCONDUCT (ARTICLE 44)
A single serious offence or gross misconduct permits immediate summary dismissal without notice or gratuity under UAE Labour Law Article 44:
  • Working intoxicated or under influence of narcotics
  • Theft, fraud, forgery, or intentional physical assault
  • Divulging confidential client technical or pricing secrets
  • Severe breach of client site life-saving safety rules
